Full screen

iPhone doesn't allow full screen from inside Safari. Add Car view to your home screen once, and it opens full screen with no Safari bars:

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (in Chrome on iPhone: tap next to the address).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  3. Keep the name Car view and tap Add.
  4. Open Car view from your home screen (sign in once if asked).

This browser didn't allow full screen. Install Car view as an app instead:

  1. Open the browser menu ⋮.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Car view from your home screen.

Until then, Focus mode hides the buttons and details so the map and speed fill the screen.
